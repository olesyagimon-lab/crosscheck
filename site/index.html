How should I photograph the delivery?

Lay out all items without stacking them. Take one sharp photo with the existing product codes facing the camera, one visible code per unit. No letter stickers are needed. Photograph the document straight-on with its product names, codes and quantities readable. This reader checks printed codes; it cannot identify unlabelled goods by appearance.

A few answers
before you start

Which documents can I use?

A clear photo or a one-page text PDF. Use an English list with Product, SKU and Quantity columns, up to five product types. The code and quantity must be on the same line. Complex invoices with extra price columns may need a simpler packing list.

Do I need to add stickers?

No. Use the product codes already printed on packages. One delivery photo avoids counting an item again in a second view.

Can this identify any object by its appearance?

No. This free local reader uses text recognition to compare printed product codes. Unlabelled objects, covered labels and uncertain readings stay unverified. A visible match does not prove that all hidden items were checked.

Where are my files sent?

Your document and photo are processed in this browser. No paid API or account is required.
